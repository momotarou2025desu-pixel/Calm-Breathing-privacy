Calm Breathing – Privacy Policy

by iogusnohin

Calm Breathing (the “App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected when you use the App, and how third-party services may process data to provide advertising.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The developer of the App does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, postal address, or phone number.

The App does not require account registration, and we do not ask you to provide personal information to use core features.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may display ads using Google advertising services (Google AdMob). For ad delivery and measurement, Google may process certain device-related information in accordance with Google’s policies.

Examples of data that may be processed by Google (depending on device settings and region) can include:

  • Advertising ID (or similar identifiers)
  • IP address (general location derived from IP may be used)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction and performance data (impressions, clicks)

The developer does not receive or store information that directly identifies you from these ad services.

You can control personalized advertising using your device settings (e.g., “Ads” / “Privacy” settings) or Google’s controls. For details, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising & Privacy

3. Data Retention

The developer does not store personal data because the App does not collect personal information. Data processed by third-party advertising providers is handled according to their retention policies.

4. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App and its operation. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. International Users

If you use the App from outside your country, third-party providers (such as Google) may process data on servers located in other countries, in accordance with their policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and will take effect when published.
