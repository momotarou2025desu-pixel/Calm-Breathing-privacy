Calm Breathing – Privacy Policy by iogusnohin

Calm Breathing (the “App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App, including information processed by third-party services used for advertising.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Developer)

The developer of the App does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, postal address, or phone number.

The App does not require account registration, and we do not ask you to provide personal information to use core features.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google advertising services (Google AdMob). To provide ads and measure performance, Google may process certain device-related information according to Google’s policies.

Depending on your device settings, region, and how ads are served, information processed by Google may include:

  • Advertising ID (or similar identifiers)
  • IP address (which may be used to derive approximate location)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, OS version, language)
  • Ad performance and interaction data (impressions, clicks, fraud prevention signals)

The developer does not receive or store information that directly identifies you from these ad services.

3. Your Choices

You can limit or reset advertising identifiers and control personalized ads from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking / Apple Advertising

For details, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising & Privacy

4. Data Retention

The developer does not retain personal data because the App does not collect personal information. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google’s policies.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect the App and its operation. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. International Users

If you use the App from outside your country, third-party providers (such as Google) may process data on servers located in other countries, in accordance with their policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and will take effect when published.
