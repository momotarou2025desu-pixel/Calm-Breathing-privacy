Calm Breathing – Privacy Policy

Calm Breathing (the “App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, uses, and shares.

1. Information the App Does NOT Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Physical address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts (address book)
  • Photos, audio, or video from your camera or microphone
  • Precise location information (GPS)

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob. While the App itself does not collect personal information, AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information to provide ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. This may include:

  • Device information (such as device model, OS version)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP (not GPS)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and related identifiers
  • Ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic information (such as crash or performance signals related to ads)

The information collected by AdMob is processed by Google in accordance with Google’s policies. If you prefer, you can manage ad personalization from your Android device settings (Ads/Privacy settings).

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To support the breathing experience, the App may store the following data locally on your device:

  • Theme color and display preferences
  • In-app settings selected by you (e.g., language, display options)
  • Session-related preferences or simple history/progress (if you use such features)

These data are used only for app functionality and are not sold by the developer.

4. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information. The App does not share personal information with third parties, except that third-party advertising (AdMob) may process data as described above to deliver ads and related services.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the developer so we can take appropriate action.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and will become effective when published.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the email address listed on the Google Play store listing for the App.
